From a small spark to a useful next step.

A clearer way to meet the FoundRy, understand the local path, and find your way back to the Tools.

The FoundRy is a locally run workbench with public source. This concept gives a visitor one calm route through what it is, how to try it, and where the public Toolbox still fits.

01 · Meet the workbench

Give the idea a home before you ask it to grow.

FoundRy keeps a purpose, a voice, references, acceptance cases, and recorded evidence together in one local project.

02 · Set it up locally

The next click is clear, even when the builder is not hosted here.

The public source repository is the doorway. The local setup guide is the map.

Take the local path

  1. Open the source. Inspect the public GitHub repository.
  2. Follow its README. Download the application and use the local setup guide.
  3. Keep your records close. Projects are saved on the builder’s computer.
Open the FoundRy source

What this page does not promise

There is no hosted builder, account sign-up, or embedded application here. A passing local check does not certify an external GPT or approve publication.

03 · Carry an idea through

Let the work show what it knows and what it still needs.

A small project can move through a practical rhythm without pretending the hard parts are finished.

Describe

Name the useful part

Write who the work is for, what it should help with, and what a useful result looks like.

Try

Record what happened

Test the work in its intended setting. An observation you have not run stays marked as not run.

Review

Notice the next pass

Readiness points to missing information and incomplete evidence. It does not independently verify either one.
